Privacy Policy

What we collect

Nothing. This app runs entirely in your browser. No personal data, usage data, or analytics are collected by our frontend.

Local storage

Your light/dark mode preference is saved in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device.

Speed Test Data

This app measures your connection by downloading and uploading temporary garbage data to Cloudflare's speed testing endpoints (`speed.cloudflare.com`). We do not intercept, store, or process this traffic. Cloudflare may log your IP address as part of standard CDN delivery requests subject to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services

Google Fonts is loaded to render typography. Google may log your IP as part of font delivery. No other third-party services are used.
