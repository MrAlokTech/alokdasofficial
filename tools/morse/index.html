Privacy Policy

What we collect

Nothing. Morse Code Studio runs entirely in your browser. Audio tones are generated locally via the Web Audio API without network calls.

Local Storage Cache

Your practice streak, character accuracy metrics, and mastered characters are stored locally on your device in standard `localStorage` (`alokdas_morse_progress`). No personal data is collected or sent to external servers.
